KORLIX Brain+Voice 1.0.2 — Spoken street-address search repair

This candidate repairs address recognition after a property is already selected and adds natural search phrases. Spoken lookups fill the address field, show Finding, and reveal results without opening the keyboard. The immersive Projection entrance remains a separate mockup and is not included in this build. Signed build identity, Apple processing and internal-group delivery require separate release verification.

On a physical iPhone or iPad, repeat the address checks in Live Conversation and Deep Think:
1. From Home, start the existing Rici conversation, wait for Listening, and ask “Can you hear me?” Confirm a spoken reply. Say “Rici, search 10 Downing Street, London SW1A 2AA.” Projection should open and begin the lookup without typing or tapping Explore. Check You said, Find destination, the populated address field, and Finding progress.
2. Review the matching full address. Say “Yes, that's it” for one candidate, or “Choose the first result” when several are shown. The destination must not be selected before confirmation. Confirm that Street View opens facing the selected location; exact facade visibility depends on coverage.
3. With that property still selected and Rici listening, say only “350 Fifth Avenue, New York, NY 10118.” A new search should start and reveal its candidates. Confirm by voice. Repeat with “Search this address: 1600 Pennsylvania Avenue NW, Washington, DC 20500.” No keyboard or Explore tap should be needed for Rici's recognized address command.
4. Dictate an address that includes an apartment or unit, city and postal code. Check that the whole original query reaches the address field. Repeat with an English spoken-number street address. If the lookup cannot verify it, the app must say so and allow a corrected address; it must not invent a result. Search while another lookup is pending, and confirm that an older response cannot replace the newer one.
5. Say “Don't search 350 Fifth Avenue,” “Look up quantum mechanics,” “Tell me about the history of 10 Downing Street,” and “Remember 350 Fifth Avenue.” Ordinary research and memory requests must not silently become destination searches. A missing or expired ASR transcript must not execute later.
6. Check an explicit address command from Home with Projection closed; check a standalone address with Projection open after selection and after close/reopen. Bare conversation at Home is not an implicit destination command. Opening Projection alone must not start a microphone; tap Talk to Rici and wait for Listening if voice is off. Keyboard dictation into the text field still uses Explore, separately from Rici's conversation.
7. Confirm that “Resee, zoom in,” “Zoom out,” “Keep zooming in,” “Stop zooming,” “Turn left,” and “Reset view” retain their behavior. Stop zooming leaves the conversation active. Test Pause, Resume and Stop conversation, quick interruption, and background/return. The voice dock and Google attribution must remain accessible in both rooms.
8. Check “Show overhead” and “Show the front of the property.” For missing Street View coverage, show unavailable coverage and leave 3D Earth available. Check directions, account and memory briefly. The visual animation concept must not appear in this candidate.

Street View is recorded imagery, not a live camera feed. Available capture dates are shown; zoom cannot create detail absent from the provider imagery.

For any failure, report installed version/build, device, voice status, the exact You said text, View control/result, and whether candidates appeared. Physical microphone-to-Projection acceptance remains required even after automated tests pass. This candidate does not submit a public App Store release.

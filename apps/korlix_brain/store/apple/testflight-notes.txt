KORLIX Brain+Voice 1.0.2 — Native Projection controls and voice routing

This build adds persistent native Back, Talk, Pause, Resume and Stop controls around Projection, plus Auto and Landscape controls. Rici handles recognized view commands before speaking their results. Requests for another address can be given in one sentence or as a two-step conversation.

On an iPhone or iPad:
1. Start Live Conversation and wait for Listening. Say “Show projection mode in landscape format.” Projection should open in landscape format with the native controls visible. Back returns to the existing conversation; Stop leaves Projection open with Talk available.
2. Say “Search 10 Downing Street, London SW1A 2AA” and confirm the matching result. Say “Can you take me to a different address?” Then give “350 Fifth Avenue, New York, NY 10118” and confirm. Give a third full address in the same conversation. Each confirmed result should replace the property without restarting voice or typing.
3. Tap Auto and rotate both directions. Test Landscape, Pause, Resume, Stop and Talk. Open the address keyboard and check that Back and Stop remain reachable. The selected property and drafts should survive layout changes.
4. Background the app while listening, then return. The microphone should be paused until you tap Resume. Swipe up to close, then reopen. If TestFlight still reports a crash, submit its crash feedback so the exact native stack can be inspected.
5. Repeat the destination sequence in Deep Think. Check Street View/Earth and continuous zoom independently of Pause/Stop conversation.

For a failed command, include the installed build, device, voice state, exact “You said” text, and displayed result. A spoken acknowledgement alone does not establish that the property changed. Native simulator coverage and physical microphone/closing behavior are separate checks.

Street View uses recorded imagery; coverage and facade visibility vary. This build is for the existing internal TestFlight group.

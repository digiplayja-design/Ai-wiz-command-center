KORLIX Brain+Voice 1.0.2 — Projection voice-control repair

This candidate addresses voice activation visibility, natural Rici name/command phrasing, and missing speech-recognition feedback. Physical iPhone/iPad microphone and WebView acceptance is still required. The release pipeline must separately confirm this candidate's signed build, Apple upload and existing-group assignment.

On a physical iPhone or iPad:
1. Open Projection with voice stopped. Without scrolling, confirm that Talk to Rici and the microphone status are visible beside the property workspace. Loading imagery alone must not start the microphone. Tap Talk to Rici, wait for Listening, and ask “Can you hear me?” Confirm a spoken reply and a second turn.
2. Say “Rici, go to San Francisco Ferry Building, San Francisco, CA.” Review the destination, then say “That’s it” for one candidate or “Choose the first one.” Confirm Street View opens facing the selected location. For a typed or keyboard-dictated address, tap Explore and confirm the result; Talk to Rici is still required for spoken view controls.
3. Say “Reese, zoom in,” “Resee, zoom in,” “Please, Rici, zoom in,” “Zoom in, Rici,” and “Rici, I want you to zoom in.” Check the actual recognized words under You said, the interpreted View control, and the resulting image movement. Check “Zoom out,” “Turn left,” “Turn right,” and “Reset view.”
4. Say “Keep zooming in on the building,” followed by “Stop zooming.” Movement must stop while Rici remains listening. Repeat in Live Conversation and Deep Think, including while a research answer is pending. A command should execute once; an older reply must not repeat or override it.
5. Say “Please, Rici, don't zoom in,” “Explain how to zoom in,” “Look up quantum mechanics,” and “Tell me a joke.” These must not move the view. If speech recognition does not return a transcript, a useful notice should explain the missing words; a stale command must not move the camera later.
6. Test Pause, Resume and Stop during connection and speech. Stop Conversation must turn off the microphone, leave imagery open, and keep Talk to Rici accessible. Test interruption, quick destination changes, touching the view during continuous zoom, and background/return. Repeat in both Projection rooms and check that Google attribution remains visible.
7. Say “Show overhead,” then “Show the front of the property.” Confirm the mode label and imagery agree. Try an address with no Street View: display unavailable coverage and leave 3D Earth available.

Street View is recorded imagery, not a live camera feed. Available capture dates are shown; exact facade visibility depends on coverage. Zoom stops at the provider's imagery limit and cannot create extra image detail.

For any failure, report the installed version/build, device, voice status, exact You said text, View control/result, and selected destination. Check PDF import/review, Camera Ask, directions and account controls briefly. This internal candidate does not submit a public App Store release.

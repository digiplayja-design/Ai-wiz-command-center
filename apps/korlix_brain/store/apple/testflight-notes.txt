KORLIX Brain+Voice 1.0.2 — Live restart and Projection voice control

This candidate keeps the conversation restart control visible after Stop and gives Rici explicit instructions for opening Projection from Home. The existing immersive entrance, Street View and spoken-address search remain included. Release delivery and physical-device acceptance are verified separately.

On a physical iPhone or iPad:
1. Start Live Conversation, wait for Listening, speak once, then tap Stop. The microphone should turn off and a prominent Start Live Conversation button should remain. Tap it and wait for Listening again. Repeat twice, including after scrolling a long transcript and navigating to Home or Saved. Start should remain visible and work without relaunching the app.
2. While the restarted Live Conversation shows Listening on Home, say exactly: “Rici can you open projection mode.” Projection should open automatically while the same voice session stays active. Rici should acknowledge the actual opening. A canceled “I cannot open it from here” reply must not remain in the written conversation or reappear after Pause/Resume.
3. Say “Close projection,” then repeat “Rici, can you open projection mode, please?” No onscreen Projection tap should be required. Try the same sequence in Deep Think. Changing voice mode while stopped must start the selected mode, and Pause/Resume must preserve the existing conversation.
4. Stop conversation inside Projection. Talk to Rici should remain usable. Tap it, wait for Listening, then say “Rici, search 10 Downing Street, London SW1A 2AA.” The lookup should start without typing. Confirm with “Yes, that's it” or “Choose the first result.” Try “Zoom in,” “Keep zooming in” and “Stop zooming.”
5. Check the two-second entrance, Skip, Close and Reduce Motion. A voice restart or a new address must not replay the entrance. Property imagery, provider attribution and voice controls must remain clear.
6. Pause a conversation, resume, and ask an ordinary follow-up. Actual accepted replies should remain in the conversation. Negative, quoted, explanatory and memory requests mentioning Projection must not open it accidentally.

For a failure, report installed version/build, device, voice status, exact You said text, View control/result, and whether Projection opened. A spoken answer alone does not prove a controller action succeeded. Physical microphone-to-Projection acceptance remains required after automated tests pass.

Street View is recorded imagery, not a live property camera. Coverage varies. This candidate is for existing internal testers and does not submit a public App Store release.

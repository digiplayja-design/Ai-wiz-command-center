KORLIX Brain+Voice 1.0.2 — Immersive Projection entrance

This candidate brings the approved Awaken → Enter → Arrive entrance into the real Command Center and Discovery Theater. Full motion lasts two seconds; Reduce Motion uses a brief fade. The existing Street View, spoken-address search and Rici conversation remain active during the decoration. Release availability and physical-device acceptance are verified separately.

On a physical iPhone or iPad:
1. Open Projection from Home with the shortcut, then repeat using “Rici, enter VR” during a conversation. Look for the hollow brain, cyan/violet depth and portal lighting, followed by a calm room. The entrance should run once per opening. Close and reopen rapidly: each new opening should get one clean entrance, without a late callback from the previous one.
2. During the entrance, try Skip entrance and Close projection. Both must respond immediately. Talk, Pause, Resume and Stop must remain accessible in their stationary dock. No voice session should start merely because the animation plays.
3. While Rici shows Listening, say “Rici, search 10 Downing Street, London SW1A 2AA.” Projection should start the lookup without typing or tapping Explore, including during the entrance. Check You said, Find destination, the original address and Finding progress. Say “Yes, that's it” for one match or “Choose the first result” when several appear.
4. With a property selected, speak “350 Fifth Avenue, New York, NY 10118.” The next lookup should start without replaying the entrance. Confirm by voice, then try “Resee, zoom in,” “Keep zooming in,” “Stop zooming,” and “Reset view.” Stop zooming leaves the conversation active. Ordinary research or memory requests must not become address searches.
5. Check both rooms with a provider already loading, ready and unavailable. The real property rectangle, provider controls, logos, links and attribution must stay fully clear. The large illustrative portal must disappear when a provider is active; surrounding room accents may continue. The animation must not move, blur, fade or replace the provider itself, and must not claim imagery is ready before it loads.
6. Turn on iOS Reduce Motion, then open Projection. Expect only a brief fade, without zoom, parallax or a flashing portal. Change the preference during an entrance and confirm it settles promptly. Background the app during entry, then return: no stuck decoration and no automatic replay. Rici and any pending address lookup must retain their normal lifecycle.
7. On a narrow phone and an iPad, keep the keyboard open and interact with the address field while the entrance runs. Text, focus, results and confirmation must remain usable. With a keyboard or screen reader, Skip entrance must have a clear label; removing it must not strand focus. Room switching, voice transcripts, answers and subsequent addresses must not replay the entrance.

Street View is recorded imagery, not a live property camera. Exact facade visibility depends on coverage. The standalone concept's synthetic house and simulated voice controls are not part of this build.

For a failure, report installed version/build, device, Reduce Motion setting, voice status, exact You said text, View control/result, and what was visible at the time. Physical microphone-to-Projection acceptance remains required after automated tests pass. This candidate does not submit a public App Store release.

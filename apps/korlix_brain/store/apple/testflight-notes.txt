KORLIX Brain+Voice 1.0.2 — VR address and Projection refresh

This candidate improves address commands and refreshes Projection availability when you close and reopen it. It retains the voice connection fix from build 16. “Explore address in 3D” and “Live camera availability” now identify separate actions, and address search and 3D imagery have separate availability messages.

Please test on a physical iPhone or iPad:
1. Start a conversation, wait for “Rici is listening,” and say “Enter VR.” Open the address controls in Projection.
2. Say “Show me 1 Market Street, San Francisco, CA 94105,” then try “Show 1 Market Street, San Francisco, CA 94105 in 3D.” Check that the complete address is preserved. Review and confirm the destination candidate before displaying it. Also try entering an address or public landmark with the keyboard.
3. Where 3D imagery is enabled, check the selected location and camera movement. This is recorded map imagery; requesting live camera availability does not establish that a live feed exists. Requests for live cameras or general research should not be treated as these address-display commands.
4. Close Projection and reopen it. Check that current address-search and imagery availability is loaded again. After a network interruption, distinguish a failed availability check from a service that is not connected. Reopening refreshes status; it does not enable a missing provider service.

Voice checks: let Rici’s welcome finish, tap Talk to Rici, allow the microphone if asked, wait for “Rici is listening,” and ask “Rici, can you hear me?” Confirm a spoken answer and a second turn. Repeat after skipping the welcome. Try Pause, Resume, and Stop while connecting and during speech. Check that Pause closes microphone audio, Resume reconnects with recent context in the same mode, and Stop clears resumable context. If listening does not appear, record the visible connection message and the installed version/build from Account.

Existing PDF review/save to Memory or Training, Camera Ask, directions, account, plan, AI GAS, welcome, ambience, and transcript controls are unchanged in this update. A brief regression check of PDF import, camera capture, and opening directions in Apple or Google Maps is useful.

Report the installed build, device, exact request, selected destination, visible status, and whether speech and imagery worked. Address search and embedded 3D imagery still depend on the deployed provider configuration. This candidate does not establish completed native-map acceptance, activate store billing, or submit an App Store release.

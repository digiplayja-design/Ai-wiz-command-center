KORLIX Brain+Voice 1.0.2 — Landscape Projection and repeated addresses

This candidate improves follow-up spoken destination searches and gives Command Center and Discovery Theater a wide landscape layout with luminous, projected controls. It retains Street View, the immersive entrance and the visible conversation restart button.

On a physical iPhone or iPad:
1. Start Live Conversation, wait for Listening, and say “Rici, open projection mode.” Say “Search 10 Downing Street, London SW1A 2AA.” Review the address and confirm with “Yes, that’s it” or “Choose the first result.” The lookup must begin without typing.
2. Keep that same conversation and Projection open. Say “Now show me 350 Fifth Avenue, New York, NY 10118.” Confirm the matching result. Then say “The next address is 1600 Pennsylvania Avenue NW, Washington, DC 20500” and confirm. The second and third properties must replace the previous destination without closing Projection, restarting voice or typing.
3. While candidate results are waiting, say “Switch the address to 10 Downing Street, London SW1A 2AA.” Only the new results should be offered. Research, quoted instructions and requests to remember an address must not change the selected property.
4. Rotate from portrait to landscape and back with a confirmed property visible and Rici listening. The wide scene, floating console buttons, address search and voice controls should be usable. The property, conversation and provider frame must remain intact; rotation must not replay the entrance or start another voice session. Check both landscape directions and Command Center / Discovery Theater.
5. In landscape, test Zoom in, Keep zooming in, Stop zooming, Street View / Earth, the address candidate buttons, Pause / Resume, Stop and Talk to Rici. Stop conversation and Close projection must remain separate. Try a second address with the keyboard as well as voice.
6. Check Skip entrance and Reduce Motion. Provider imagery and attribution must remain clear. Stop and restart Live Conversation twice, including after closing Projection; Start Live Conversation must stay available. Repeat the spoken-address sequence in Deep Think.

For a failure, report installed version/build, device, orientation, voice status, exact You said text, and View control/result. A spoken acknowledgement alone does not prove the view changed. These checks establish physical-device behavior separately from automated tests.

Street View is recorded imagery. Coverage and facade visibility vary. This candidate is for the existing internal TestFlight group.

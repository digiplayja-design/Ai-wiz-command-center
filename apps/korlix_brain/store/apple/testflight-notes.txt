KORLIX Brain+Voice 1.0.1 (10)

Transcript messages now have Save, Share and Delete actions. Save keeps a secure copy for your account on this device; open Saved messages from Talk or My Brain. Share opens the system share sheet. Delete removes the transcript and its saved copy after confirmation, without changing Brain memories.

Live Conversation remains the fast default with Rici. Deep Think is still optional. No new prices or minute allowances are enabled in this build.

Please test: save both your messages and Rici’s answers, close/reopen the app, open Saved messages, share on iPhone/iPad, cancel and confirm deletion, sign out/in to verify account separation, and confirm the Stop button stays available.

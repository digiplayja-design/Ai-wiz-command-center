KORLIX Brain+Voice 1.0.2 — voice controls and property Street View

Confirmed typed or spoken destinations now request recorded Street View from a nearby outdoor camera position facing the selected location. 3D Earth is an explicit alternative. Street View is recorded imagery, not a live camera; available dates are shown by the provider. Coverage, visibility and the exact facade vary by location. No coverage must show an unavailable message without silently switching to an overhead view.

On a physical iPhone or iPad, tap Talk to Rici and wait for Listening:
1. Say “Rici, go to San Francisco Ferry Building, San Francisco, CA.” Review the destination and say “That’s it” for one candidate or “Choose the first one.” The confirmed address must open Street View, with the view facing the selected location. Repeat with a typed or keyboard-dictated address, then submit Explore and confirm the result.
2. Say “Reese, zoom in,” “Rici. Zoom in,” “Zoom in on the property,” “Zoom in a little bit,” and “Can you zoom in for me?” The You said panel should show the recognized words, View control should show Zoom in, and the image should change when the provider permits it.
3. Say “Keep zooming in on the building,” followed by “Stop zooming.” Try “Keep zooming out,” “Hold this view,” “Turn left,” “Turn right,” and “Reset view.” Stop zooming must leave Rici listening. Repeated commands work up to the available imagery zoom limit; they cannot create extra image detail.
4. Say “Show overhead,” then “Show the front of the property” or “Show Street View.” Confirm the mode label changes correctly and switching works while the current imagery is loading or unavailable. Try an address with no Street View: show the coverage message and leave 3D Earth available.
5. In Deep Think, ask an ordinary question with a slow answer. While it is pending, say “Stop zooming.” The camera must stop promptly; an older answer must not repeat or override the newer command. Repeat in Live Conversation and check one action and one acknowledgement per direct command.
6. Interrupt speech, change destinations quickly, touch the view during continuous zoom, pause the conversation, background the app, and return. Old movement and late lookup results must not resume or replace the current destination. ASR failures should show a useful speech-recognition message in Projection.
7. Ask “Look up quantum mechanics,” “Tell me a joke,” and “Is there a live camera feed?” Ordinary conversation must not move the view, and live-camera availability must be described honestly.

Voice regression: ask “Rici, can you hear me?” and confirm a spoken reply and a second turn. Test Pause, Resume and Stop while connecting and speaking. Stop Conversation must turn off the microphone; Stop Zooming must preserve the conversation. Repeat in both Projection rooms.

Check PDF import/review, Camera Ask, directions and account controls briefly. Report the installed version/build, device, exact request shown under You said, selected destination and result. This candidate requires physical-device acceptance and does not submit an App Store release.

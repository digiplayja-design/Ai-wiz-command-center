KORLIX Brain+Voice 1.0.2 — spoken destinations and continuous zoom

This candidate connects recognized spoken VR commands directly to the existing destination lookup and confirmation flow. Rici no longer has to choose a map tool before a supported spoken destination or camera command can run. It retains the voice connection fix and address/Projection refresh work from builds 16 and 17.

On a physical iPhone or iPad, start a conversation and wait for Rici to listen:
1. Say “Open VR and go to San Francisco Ferry Building, San Francisco, CA.” Check that the complete spoken destination appears. Review the candidate, then say “That’s it” for a single result or “Choose the first one.” Confirm that the selected location is displayed without typing.
2. Say “Go to 1 Market Street, San Francisco, CA 94105.” Check that the complete address is preserved and the candidate still needs confirmation. Also test a typed address.
3. Say “Zoom in” several times, then “Keep zooming in.” Say “Stop zooming” or “Stop moving the view” to hold the camera while keeping Rici listening. Try “Keep zooming out,” “Hold this view,” “Rotate left,” “Rotate right,” and “Reset view.” A Stop Zoom button is also available.
4. Repeated commands have no count limit. Continuous movement stops at the closest or farthest navigation bound. Closer navigation cannot create detail absent from the recorded imagery; this is not a live camera feed.
5. During continuous movement, touch the map, choose another destination, pause the conversation, background the app, and return. Old camera movement must stay stopped. A delayed earlier address result must not replace a newer destination.
6. After selecting a place, ask an ordinary question such as “Look up quantum mechanics” or “Tell me a joke.” Rici should continue the conversation without changing the destination. Confirm one action and one spoken acknowledgement per direct camera command.

Voice regression: ask “Rici, can you hear me?” and confirm a spoken reply and a second turn. Test Pause, Resume, and Stop while connecting and speaking. Stop Zoom must preserve the conversation; Stop Conversation must close microphone audio and clear resumable context. Repeat after skipping the welcome and in both Live Conversation and Deep Think modes.

Check PDF import/review, Camera Ask, directions, and account controls briefly. Report the installed version/build, device, exact spoken request, selected destination, visible status, and whether speech and imagery worked. This candidate still requires physical-device acceptance and does not submit an App Store release.

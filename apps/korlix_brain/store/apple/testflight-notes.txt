New: a short, excited Rici welcome, bundled for instant offline playback. It plays once on first use; use Skip introduction or Replay welcome on Home. Starting voice, dictation, navigating away or backgrounding stops it. Music stays quiet during the welcome and resumes afterward if you enabled it.

Please listen on iPhone speaker and Bluetooth, test Silent Mode, Skip/Replay, close/reopen, and immediate Talk to Rici. Check that the introduction never overlaps the microphone or resumes after you skip or leave the app. Confirm Rici says her name correctly in Live, Deep Think and memory acknowledgments. Existing transcript Save/Share/Delete and music volume persistence remain available.

# Button climber artwork

The replacement uses original photographic-style human artwork generated with the built-in image-generation tool (not the CLI/API fallback).

Final project asset: `assets/characters/button_climbers/realistic-people-v2.png`

The original 1536 × 1024 RGBA output is preserved unchanged. The app samples its anatomical body regions and animates them with planted contact targets and inverse kinematics. It is an animated illustration, not live video of real people. No AI generation runs in the customer app.

## Final generation prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: transparent human cutout texture sheet for a miniature UI climbing animation. Create ONE wide landscape PNG, 1536 by 1024 composition, with exactly two separate full-body real adult people, a man on the left half and a woman on the right half, both evenly lit by soft neutral photographic studio lighting. Genuinely transparent alpha background, no floor, no environment, no ground shadows. They must look like high-resolution photographs of real people, not illustrations, not cartoon, not chibi, not toys, not game avatars. Normal adult anatomy and proportions, approximately 7.5 head heights tall; realistic small heads, skin texture, detailed hands and fabric. Man: fit adult in his thirties, medium brown skin, short dark hair, subtle stubble, muted dark teal fitted short-sleeve athletic T-shirt, fitted charcoal climbing trousers, dark grey practical sneakers. Woman: fit adult in her thirties, warm tan skin, dark hair tied closely in a short ponytail, muted plum fitted short-sleeve athletic T-shirt, fitted charcoal climbing trousers, dark grey practical sneakers. Both face mostly forward with only a slight 10-degree turn toward image right, neutral friendly face. IMPORTANT POSE FOR RIGGING: both stand in a relaxed anatomical A pose, each arm straight and angled diagonally down and OUT 35 degrees from their torso, with clear transparent gaps between arms and waist. Hands relaxed with fingers together. Legs straight, slightly spread, both shoes fully visible and separated. No bent arms, no hands on hips, no crossed limbs. Entire bodies including heads, fingers and soles inside frame with 40 pixel safe margins. Equal full-body scale and baseline; each person stays in their own half with a generous transparent gap between them. No logos, no lettering, no borders, no accessories, no ropes or climbing equipment.

## Full description

Good words. Always within reach.

KORLIX COPY Box gives your ideas, useful replies, notes, and reusable text a space
of their own. Start with independent colored boxes, add more as you need them,
and find the right words when it is time to use them.

WRITE YOUR WAY

Edit several boxes in one scrolling workspace. Give each box a title and folder,
choose its color, and keep favorites close. A focus editor gives longer thoughts
more room. Copy one box or copy all your active boxes in workspace order.

START WITH SOMETHING USEFUL

Choose a client follow-up, project update, customer support reply, meeting recap,
social caption, or proposal brief. Add fields such as {{name}} and {{date}}, fill
in the details, and preview the finished text before copying it. Your reusable
original stays intact.

KEEP A WAY BACK

Autosave keeps ordinary edits on your device. Replacements can retain previous
text versions, and deleted boxes go to a recoverable trash area. Export a JSON
backup to move your boxes between installations. Preview an import before adding
new boxes, or export current text for use elsewhere.

REFINE WITH YOUR KORLIX ACCOUNT

Connect the same email and password you use for KORLIX AI to polish, shorten,
summarize, or reshape a selected box. AI refinement requires a connection and is
subject to your account's current usage limits. Review a proposed draft before
applying it, save it as a new box, keep the original, or report the output.

A SPACE THAT FEELS LIKE YOURS

A black-and-white KORLIX emblem, softly colored writing cards, light and dark
appearance, device text scaling, and straightforward controls keep the focus on
your words.

Local writing does not require sign-in. Local boxes and signed-in workspaces are
separate. Boxes are stored on your device; automatic cross-device synchronization
is not included. Exported backups contain readable text, including history and
trash. AI refinement sends your chosen text to KORLIX and OpenAI after consent.

## Reviewer path

1. Open the app. Local writing is available without a reviewer account.
2. Enter text in two boxes, use Save All, then restart to verify persistence.
3. Copy one box, Copy All Boxes, and exercise system sharing.
4. Use a starter template, fill its fields, and copy the preview.
5. Save a JSON backup, then import it with the preview/confirmation flow.
6. Use test KORLIX credentials supplied separately in the secure review fields to
   exercise optional AI refinement, consent, report output, and account requests.

Do not put reviewer passwords, production tokens, signing credentials, or real
customer text in this file, screenshots, or repository.

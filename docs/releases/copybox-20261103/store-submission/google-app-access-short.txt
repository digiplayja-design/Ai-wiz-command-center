Local writing works without login. For AI, sign in under Settings with the supplied review account. Add nonsensitive sample text in the account workspace. Choose Refine > Polish, read the AI and model-training notice, then select Allow AI and training. Use local only declines. Review the draft before applying it. Settings > Revoke AI consent resets future permission for this account on this device; account deletion is also in Settings. No purchase or one-time code is needed.

Some functionality is restricted: optional AI refinement requires a KORLIX account. Local writing, copying, templates, search, organization, backup/import and trash recovery work without signing in.

Use the dedicated reviewer email and password entered in the private app-access credential fields. Open Settings > Connect KORLIX and sign in. The account workspace is separate from the guest workspace. Add nonsensitive sample text to a box after signing in, select Improve with AI, and choose a writing action.

Before the first AI request, the app asks permission to send the selected box's full text and instruction to KORLIX and OpenAI. It also explains retention in the account's AI history. Allowing this consent enables the request. Declining leaves local writing available. Review the proposed output before applying it or saving it as a new box. Report this output opens the report form.

Settings includes Request KORLIX account deletion, account-deletion information, privacy, terms, support and the app version. The deletion request concerns the shared KORLIX account; it does not instantly erase the account or remotely clear local boxes.

The app has no COPY Box checkout or purchase flow. The supplied review account must have sufficient AI allowance and an available device slot. Use the supplied account rather than creating a new one during review. Support: support@korlixdeveloper.com.

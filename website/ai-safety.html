KORLIX AI is intended for adults to create, learn, organize and communicate responsibly. These rules apply to prompts, uploaded material, generated text, images, audio, video, music, code and AI-assisted business workflows.

Prohibited requests and content

  • Child sexual abuse or exploitation, grooming, sexualized depictions of minors and child sexual abuse material, including synthetic or AI-generated material.
  • Pornographic or sexually explicit content intended for sexual gratification; non-consensual intimate imagery, sexual violence, deceptive sexual impersonation, threats, hateful abuse or targeted harassment.
  • Instructions or assistance for violent wrongdoing, serious harm, extremist recruitment, self-harm facilitation, fraud, scams, malware or unauthorized access.
  • Impersonation intended to deceive, fabricated evidence presented as authentic, privacy violations, unauthorized use of likeness or voice, and infringement of others' rights.
  • Attempts to bypass safeguards, account limits, permission controls or payment requirements.

Report an AI output

Use the in-app Report Output action on an AI result to send a report without leaving the app. Choose the relevant reason and include enough context to explain the concern. You can also contact support@korlixdeveloper.com with the feature and approximate time or result reference. Do not forward illegal imagery. Report Social content through its content/member report controls.

Reports may include the selected output, your reason, account/request identifiers and related context needed for review. Authorized reviewers can use this information to assess the report, restrict content or accounts and improve safeguards. Contact support if you believe a restriction was mistaken. See the Privacy Policy for report handling and retention.

Review before relying or publishing

AI can fabricate facts, omit context, misread receipts or produce harmful or unsuitable suggestions. Check important claims against reliable sources and obtain qualified advice for consequential decisions. Do not treat generated financial, tax, legal, medical or security content as verified professional advice. Label synthetic media where required and do not present it as proof that a real event happened.

Music, images and voice

Only submit material you have permission to use. Do not request unauthorized copies of protected songs, lyrics or samples, deceptive artist/voice impersonation, or intimate content involving a person without consent. Permission to use the app is not a guarantee of copyright ownership or commercial clearance. BabyBlend uses permitted adult references for fictional portraits, not real-child identification or genetic predictions.

Child safety and urgent danger

Our Child Safety Standards prohibit child sexual abuse and exploitation across the service. For immediate danger, contact local emergency services or the relevant child-protection authority. KORLIX support and AI are not emergency services.

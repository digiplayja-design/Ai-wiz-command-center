AI Safety Policy

Korlix AI is designed to help users create, learn, plan, and ask questions responsibly. Users must not use Korlix AI to create harmful, illegal, abusive, deceptive, or unsafe content.

Reporting AI Output

Users can report AI-generated output inside the app using the Report Output feature. Reports may be reviewed to improve safety and enforce app rules.

Restricted Content

Korlix AI may restrict content involving illegal instructions, self-harm, sexual exploitation, hateful conduct, harassment, violent wrongdoing, fraud, malware, impersonation, and unauthorized copyrighted content.

Music and Media Safety

Users may not request unauthorized copying of songs, artist impersonation, copyrighted lyrics, unauthorized samples, or deceptive audio content. Music Production features are coming soon and will be subject to additional restrictions.

Contact

To report safety concerns, contact: support@korlixdeveloper.com

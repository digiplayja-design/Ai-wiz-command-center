Effective date: October 7, 2026

KORLIX AI is for people aged 16 and older to create, learn, organize and communicate responsibly. Users aged 16 or 17 need permission from a parent or legal guardian. AI characters are artificial intelligence, not real people or a substitute for trusted adults or professional support. Keep passwords out of AI conversations. Share other personal information only when needed for the feature and when you have permission to use it. Use Report Output or our support contact to report unsafe responses. These rules apply to prompts, uploaded material, generated text, images, audio, video, music, code and AI-assisted business workflows.

Prohibited requests and content

  • Child sexual abuse or exploitation, grooming, sexualized depictions of minors and child sexual abuse material, including synthetic or AI-generated material.
  • Pornographic or sexually explicit content intended for sexual gratification; non-consensual intimate imagery, sexual violence, deceptive sexual impersonation, threats, hateful abuse or targeted harassment.
  • Instructions or assistance for violent wrongdoing, serious harm, extremist recruitment, self-harm facilitation, fraud, scams, malware or unauthorized access.
  • Impersonation intended to deceive, fabricated evidence presented as authentic, privacy violations, unauthorized use of likeness or voice, and infringement of others' rights.
  • Attempts to bypass safeguards, account limits, permission controls or payment requirements.

Report an AI output

Use the in-app Report Output action on an AI result to send a report without leaving the app. Choose the relevant reason and include enough context to explain the concern. You can also contact support@korlixdeveloper.com with the feature and approximate time or result reference. Do not forward illegal imagery. Report Social content through its content/member report controls.

Reports may include the selected output, your reason, account/request identifiers and related context needed for review. Authorized reviewers can use this information to assess the report, restrict content or accounts and improve safeguards. Contact support if you believe a restriction was mistaken. See the Privacy Policy for report handling and retention.

Review before relying or publishing

AI can fabricate facts, omit context, misread receipts or produce harmful or unsuitable suggestions. Check important claims against reliable sources and obtain qualified advice for consequential decisions. Do not treat generated financial, tax, legal, medical or security content as verified professional advice. Label synthetic media where required and do not present it as proof that a real event happened.

  • Receipts, Bookkeeping and Tax Prep: check original documents, amounts, dates, currency, categorization and business purpose. An AI classification does not establish tax deductibility. Tax Prep organizes records; it does not calculate tax liability or refunds or submit a return.
  • Workforce and FieldProof: review attendance exceptions, reminders, photos and location evidence with the affected person before a consequential workplace decision. Missing data is not proof of misconduct. A photo, timestamp or hash does not independently prove identity, consent, workmanship or legal authenticity.
  • Contracts and business tools: verify Contract Radar opportunities, qualifications, amendments and deadlines with the issuing source. Have qualified people review contracts and consequential financial, employment, legal or security decisions. AI-generated code and websites need security, accessibility and operational review before production use.
  • Learning, news and podcasts: Study and Learn, Discover, Live Convo and The Pod and You can contain incorrect or outdated information. AI panelists are synthetic speakers; discussion, citations or confident delivery do not establish a claim's truth or a professional qualification.

Safe automation and memory

Review recipients, permissions, instructions and limits before enabling an email, phone, scheduling, workforce or business automation. Authorization to draft is not authorization to send; an enabled automatic rule can act within its configured permissions without a new approval for each action. Respect opt-outs, required recording and artificial-voice disclosures, and the permissions of everyone affected. Stop rules and disconnect accounts you no longer authorize.

Saved agent memory, training files and voice instructions can influence later responses and may themselves contain mistakes. Only provide information you have permission to use, review and correct saved material where controls are offered, and do not use memory as a password or secrets manager. Imported files, emails and webpages may contain malicious instructions; do not follow requests to reveal credentials, send money or override security controls merely because an AI repeats them.

Music, images and voice

Only submit material you have permission to use. Do not request unauthorized copies of protected songs, lyrics or samples, deceptive artist/voice impersonation, or intimate content involving a person without consent. Permission to use the app is not a guarantee of copyright ownership or commercial clearance. BabyBlend uses permitted adult references for fictional portraits, not real-child identification or genetic predictions.

These rules apply to Imagine a Picture, Improve My Picture, logo creation, virtual try-ons, Music Studio, meeting recordings, livestreams and Social media. Do not use edited images to fabricate evidence, defame someone or falsely imply endorsement. A virtual outfit or body visualization is not a fit guarantee or a medical assessment. Obtain required consent before uploading another person's photo or voice, and review the Music Production Terms before distributing a generated track.

Child safety and urgent danger

Our Child Safety Standards prohibit child sexual abuse and exploitation across the service. For immediate danger, contact local emergency services or the relevant child-protection authority. KORLIX support and AI are not emergency services.

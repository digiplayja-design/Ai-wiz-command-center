CLEAR SIGNALS. CLEAR EXPECTATIONS.

What verified means.

KORLIX Verified Business means an administrator reviewed the owner's connection to the listed business, including business email, phone and suitable ownership evidence, and the optional membership is active. It does not guarantee service quality, legal compliance, licensing, insurance or customer satisfaction.

A free listing stays free.

Anyone with a KORLIX account can submit a basic business listing. Approval does not require payment. Verification is a separate, optional application; a paid subscription alone cannot activate a badge.

Review first. Payment second.

After approval, owners can choose $4.99 per month or $49 per year in USD. Membership renews automatically until canceled. Cancel renewal in KORLIX → Utility → Business Directory → My Businesses; paid benefits remain until the paid period ends unless verification is revoked or a payment is disputed or refunded. The free listing remains, subject to directory rules.

Material changes to the business identity or contact information require another review. Unpaid, expired, suspended or revoked memberships do not display a badge. Sponsored placements, if introduced, will be labeled separately.

Evidence stays private.

Ownership evidence is visible only to the listing owner and authorized directory reviewers. Do not upload passwords, payment-card details or unrelated personal documents. Redact unnecessary identifiers. Public company pictures are separate from verification evidence.

Directory rules

Submit only businesses you own or are authorized to represent. Use accurate information and pictures you have permission to publish. No impersonation, scams, illegal offers, misleading claims or duplicate spam. KORLIX may request changes or remove listings and verification. Use “Report listing” to raise a concern.

For billing questions, refunds, verification appeals or evidence-removal requests, contact support@korlixdeveloper.com. Membership cancellation does not automatically refund completed payments. Applicable rights and the disclosed checkout terms continue to apply.

Effective date: October 7, 2026

Korlix Developer prohibits child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) throughout KORLIX AI, including Social, messages, groups, calls, profiles, albums, Discover videos, voice notes, games, livestreams and AI-generated content. KORLIX is for users aged 16 and older. Users aged 16 or 17 need permission from a parent or legal guardian. Our protections against child sexual abuse and exploitation apply to everyone under 18, including eligible teen users. Do not create an account if you are under 16. A higher age requirement imposed by applicable law or a connected service still applies.

Zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation

Do not create, upload, request, solicit, distribute, promote or facilitate child sexual abuse material (CSAM), sexualized depictions of minors, grooming, sextortion, child trafficking or any sexual exploitation of children. This prohibition includes fictional, altered, synthetic and AI-generated material depicting minors in sexual situations, and using the service to arrange or enable abuse elsewhere.

Do not use image enhancement, virtual try-on, BabyBlend, music, voice or other generation tools to sexualize a minor or create an exploitative likeness. An age label, fictional character, claimed permission or private audience does not make prohibited material acceptable. Attempts to move abusive contact to another app or to hide it with Auto Dump remain violations.

Age and teen safety

Signup uses a self-declared age range and, for 16- or 17-year-olds, a guardian-permission acknowledgment. This is not independent age or guardian verification. Do not misrepresent your age or help an under-16 person bypass eligibility rules. If you believe an account belongs to someone under 16, report the account through the contact route below so we can assess it and take appropriate action.

Eligible teens should review privacy and audience choices with a trusted adult, avoid sharing their school, exact location, contact details or intimate material with people they do not know, and use report and block controls for unwanted contact. AI characters are not real people, guardians or emergency responders. Guardian permission does not waive these safety standards or applicable privacy rights.

How to report

Use the in-app Social report controls for a member, message, topic or reply, or Report Output for an AI result. You may also email support@korlixdeveloper.com with the subject Child Safety — Korlix AI. Identify the account, content reference, feature and approximate time, and describe the concern. Do not download, attach or redistribute suspected illegal images or videos in order to report them.

Our response

We assess reports, act to remove or restrict identified violating content and accounts, preserve relevant information where appropriate or legally required, and report CSAM or related unlawful conduct to the appropriate authority when the applicable legal reporting requirements are met. We cooperate with lawful child-safety requests. Access to report information is limited to people who need it for the investigation and required response. Automated checks and user reports cannot guarantee detection of every violation.

Child-safety contact

Korlix Developer's child-safety contact route is support@korlixdeveloper.com, subject Child Safety — Korlix AI. Use this route for policy questions, authority requests or follow-up information. Do not include passwords or unnecessary personal information.

Immediate danger and related policies

If a child is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services or the appropriate child-protection authority. KORLIX reports and email are not emergency response services. See our Community Guidelines, AI Safety Policy and Privacy Policy for additional rules and data handling.

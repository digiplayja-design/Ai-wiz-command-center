Effective date: September 29, 2026

These rules apply to KORLIX Social profiles, forums, direct messages, groups, attachments, calls and other content shared through KORLIX AI. You must be 18 or older and agree to the community rules before participating. The Terms of Use, AI Safety Policy and Child Safety Standards also apply.

Content and behavior we prohibit

  • Child sexual abuse and exploitation, grooming, trafficking, sexualized content involving minors and child sexual abuse material, including synthetic material.
  • Pornographic or sexually explicit content, sexual violence, non-consensual intimate content, sexual harassment or sharing another person's intimate imagery without permission.
  • Credible threats, graphic violence, extremist recruitment, encouragement of violence or self-harm, hateful attacks, bullying, targeted harassment and evasion of another person's block.
  • Scams, fraud, deceptive impersonation, spam, unwanted repeated contact, malicious links or unauthorized account access.
  • Doxxing, disclosure of private/confidential information without authority, infringement of intellectual-property rights or illegal transactions.

Respect contact and audience controls

Use accepted connections and group invitations as intended. A connection or group membership is not consent to harassment, marketing or recording. Do not add or contact people to evade restrictions. Obtain required permissions before recording a call, using someone else's photo or sending business communications. Profiles and forum contributions can be member-visible even if your profile is hidden from People.

Report and block

Use the report option associated with the relevant member, message, forum topic or reply to send a concern. Use member block controls to stop an unwanted connection. Group members can leave a group and owners can manage membership. If an option is unavailable or the issue involves account access, email support@korlixdeveloper.com with the member/content reference and a description. Do not send illegal imagery.

Moderation and review

We review reports and may remove or restrict content, suspend accounts, limit features or refer relevant matters to authorities. Severe violations can result in immediate restrictions. Reported content and necessary safety records may be retained to investigate abuse. Blocking or deleting the original item does not necessarily remove a report snapshot. Automated checks and user reports do not guarantee detection of every violation.

If you believe a decision was mistaken, contact support with the relevant account or decision reference and the reason you request review. Do not retaliate against reporters or submit knowingly false reports. For immediate danger contact emergency services; community support is not an emergency response channel.

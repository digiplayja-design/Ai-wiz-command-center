You can request deletion of your KORLIX AI account and associated data from Korlix Developer through either route below. You do not need to reinstall the app or buy a subscription to send an email request.

1. Request in the app

Sign in, open the Korlix Account panel and select Request account deletion. Submit the request using the account you want deleted. If you cannot sign in or that control is unavailable in your version, use email.

2. Request by email

Email support@korlixdeveloper.com with the subject Delete Korlix AI Account. Send from your account email where possible and identify the KORLIX account concerned. Do not send your password, authentication codes, full card details or copies of sensitive identity documents in the initial email. If you no longer control the email address, explain that so support can arrange appropriate verification.

What happens next

The in-app action creates a deletion request for review; it does not instantly erase the account. Support verifies ownership, clarifies any organization records or retention exceptions, and explains the expected timeframe. We process verified requests without undue delay and within any deadline required by applicable law. We notify you when processing is completed or explain a material delay. An acknowledgment alone is not confirmation of completed deletion.

Data covered by account deletion

The request covers your account/profile and associated KORLIX-held content, including saved memories and agent data, Social profile and account-associated content, business and CRM records, Bookkeeping and Tax Prep data, uploaded receipts and files, studio projects and media references, workforce records within your authority, and saved meeting recordings. Relevant storage files must be addressed as well as database records. Applicable provider-side deletion requests are handled as part of the process.

Shared organization records, another user's independently held content and records subject to a legitimate retention exception may need different handling. We explain what is removed, de-identified or retained. Deletion cannot recall recipient copies, public reposts or exports you have downloaded. Chat/history caches stored only on your device must be cleared on that device; deleting cloud data does not remotely erase every local copy.

Information that may be retained

Limited transaction, tax/accounting, security, fraud, moderation or dispute records may be retained where a specific legal obligation or legitimate security reason requires them. We restrict their use and keep them only for the applicable obligation or unresolved matter. Backups may expire later than active records under the relevant backup process. Ask support for the categories, reason and applicable retention period for any exception in your case. A feature's audit trail does not justify keeping all account data indefinitely.

Delete selected data without closing your account

Where the feature provides removal controls, you can delete saved notes, projects, reports, photos, recordings or other items individually. Removing a source photo, attachment link or financial entry may not remove derived results, original storage files or minimal audit/usage receipts. Contact support for deletion assistance with an item you cannot remove yourself.

Cancel subscriptions separately

Before closing your account, export records you need and manage recurring subscriptions with the provider that bills you. For Google Play open Subscriptions. Account deletion and uninstalling the app do not automatically cancel a subscription or create a refund. See Subscription Terms and the retention policy.

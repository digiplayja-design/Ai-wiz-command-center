About Korlix AI

Korlix AI is built for users who want a premium AI assistant experience with character-based interaction, useful answers, document creation, saved history, and creative tools.

Chee Chai Chee is the first Korlix AI character. More characters are planned for future releases.

Plans

Basic: Free access with daily limits, ads, and one character.

Pro: Higher limits, up to three characters, saved history, PDF/export tools, and reduced or no ads.

Ultra Premium: All available characters, beta access, premium tools, no ads, and limited video generation.

Enterprise: Custom team access, admin controls, onboarding, priority support, and custom limits.

Music Production

Updated October 7, 2026. KORLIX is for people aged 16 and older; users aged 16 or 17 need parent or guardian permission. Feature availability depends on your platform, version, plan and enabled services.

Your privacy choices

  • AI sharing: accept or decline the provider and data categories requested by a feature. In Korlix Account → Legal & Privacy, reset saved choices for this account on this device. Stop active sessions and pause enabled automations separately.
  • Photos, microphone and location: choose permissions in your device or browser settings. Workforce captures location for your chosen attendance action; it does not continuously track you in the background.
  • Saved data: use the feature’s removal control or request account and data deletion. An AI permission reset, a hidden message and a deleted account have different effects.
  • Help or privacy request: contact support@korlixdeveloper.com.

A few important feature details

Receipt Wiz
Free for signed-in users, subject to disclosed allowances. Finance inboxes share one receipt record. The vault uses your account access; it has no separate password. Save original files separately when you need an independent copy. Receipt privacy →
Workforce
Authorized managers can see submitted attendance photos and location stamps. Evidence expires after the workspace’s selected period; attendance records remain. No facial identification or matching is performed by this workflow. Workforce privacy →
Social Auto Dump
Hides messages in your own history. It does not erase the shared message, attachments, other members’ histories or downloaded copies. Social privacy →
AI and automations
AI results can be wrong. Approved content may be processed by named external providers. Enabled email or other server automations can continue after you close the app; pause them in their feature controls. AI processing →

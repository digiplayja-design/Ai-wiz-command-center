Effective date: October 7, 2026

Music Studio provides AI music creation where the feature and the required Music Production entitlement are available. Access depends on your app version, platform, region, plan and current offer. It is not automatically included in every Basic, Pro, Ultra Premium or Enterprise plan.

Ideas, lyrics and processing

After AI-processing permission, submitted ideas, lyrics and settings are sent to MusicAPI.ai. KORLIX saves your draft, generation status, favorites and returned track information. See the Privacy Policy for handling of provider links and deletion.

The in-feature voice assistant can help prepare editable lyrics and a music recipe. A voice draft or spoken approval does not generate or save a song. Review the fields in Music Studio and use its explicit creation confirmation before submitting to the music provider. Listening to a track and talking to the assistant use separate controls; do not assume the assistant has listened to or evaluated a recording.

Allowance and availability

Review the allowance and cost displayed before submitting. A request reserves allowance during submission and becomes used allowance when accepted by the provider. An uncertain request can remain reserved while its outcome is recovered; retrying should recover that request. Removing a result does not automatically refund accepted generation usage. Provider delays, limits and safety restrictions can affect availability.

Live voice assistance uses the applicable LIVE CONVO allowance; creating music uses the Music Production creation allowance. These are separate from one another and from the main subscription. A draft is not a promise of a particular duration, arrangement, voice, mix or sound. Do not submit repeated creation requests while a previous request's result is uncertain; check its status and recovery controls first.

Rights and responsible use

Only submit lyrics, references and other content you are authorized to use. Do not request unauthorized song copies, samples, deceptive voice/artist impersonation or prohibited content. Generated music can resemble existing works and can contain errors. Check the applicable offer and provider terms, licenses and third-party rights before commercial use. KORLIX does not guarantee originality, exclusivity, copyright registration or clearance.

Purchasing an allowance does not itself clear a sample, composition, recording, name, likeness or voice that belongs to someone else. Obtain any permissions needed for distribution, synchronization, advertising or other commercial use, and follow the rules of the destination platform. Do not present a synthetic performance as an actual artist's performance or endorsement. KORLIX does not promise royalty income, distributor acceptance, monetization eligibility, voice cloning, mastering or ownership of third-party material.

Keep and remove your work

Provider-hosted audio links can expire and are not a permanent archive. Download files you want to keep. Removing a creation clears its saved idea and links from KORLIX while a minimal request/usage receipt may remain. It does not recall downloaded/shared copies or automatically delete provider-held files; contact support for account or provider-deletion assistance.

Purchases and cancellations are covered by the Subscription Terms. The AI Safety Policy also applies.

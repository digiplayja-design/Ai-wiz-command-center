Inventory Studio and Bookkeeping require Enterprise. KORLIX 2MEETU scheduling is available to signed-in users on every plan, including free Basic. AI voice and other metered services remain subject to their own allowances.

Pro and Ultra Premium renew automatically each month until canceled. Sign in and open Settings → Plans & Billing to view plans or manage existing web billing, including payment details, invoices and cancellation. New purchases and plan changes are offered only when the relevant checkout is enabled. Cancellation keeps access through your paid period. Stripe shows the final charge before you pay.

AI GAS, Music Studio and the optional Business Directory verification membership are separate purchases. A generation may use more than one credit. Daily and monthly allowances have separate limits; plans do not promise unlimited use. App-store prices and billing routes can differ.

For billing or refund requests, contact support@korlixdeveloper.com with your account email and transaction reference. Never send full card details.

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 2026

Korlix AI is operated by Korlix Developer. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when users access Korlix AI.

Information We Collect

We may collect account information such as email address, authentication details, subscription status, selected AI character, usage data, saved generation history, reports submitted by users, device/app information, and support communications.

AI Prompts and Outputs

When users ask questions or request generated content, Korlix AI processes prompts and AI outputs to provide the service, save user history, enforce usage limits, improve safety, and support user-requested features.

Subscriptions, Ads, and Payments

Korlix AI may offer free, paid, and enterprise plans. Payment processing may be handled by Google Play or approved third-party services. We do not directly collect full payment card details inside the app.

Music and Video Features

Video generation may be available with controlled limits for Ultra Premium and Enterprise users. Music Production is a coming soon paid add-on and may be subject to separate credits, limits, provider availability, and content restrictions.

How We Use Information

We use information to operate Korlix AI, authenticate users, save history, enforce limits, provide support, detect misuse, process reports, manage subscriptions, improve app reliability, and comply with legal obligations.

Data Sharing

We may share limited data with service providers necessary to operate the app, including hosting, database, AI model providers, analytics, crash reporting, advertising, and payment/subscription services.

Account Deletion

Users may request account deletion through the app or through the Delete Account page. Some records may be retained when required for legal, billing, fraud prevention, security, or compliance purposes.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact: support@korlixdeveloper.com

Third-Party AI Processing

When you choose an AI-powered feature and give permission in the app, Korlix may send the content you select to third-party AI providers that process it to produce the result you requested. Depending on the feature, those providers may include OpenAI, Kling AI, and MusicAPI.ai.

The content may include:

  • typed text, questions, instructions, and prompts;
  • images, photos, and camera content you select;
  • files and documents you attach;
  • voice, audio, speech, and transcripts used in LIVE CONVO; and
  • agent training and long-term memories that you explicitly approve.

Korlix asks for explicit permission in the app before sending these categories to a newly requested provider. You may decline, in which case the AI request is not sent. Non-AI portions of Korlix remain available. A new permission request is shown when the disclosure changes or when a new provider or data category is needed.

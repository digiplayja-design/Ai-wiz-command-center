Subscription Terms

Korlix AI may offer Basic, Pro, Ultra Premium, and Enterprise access.

Basic

Basic is a free plan with daily generation limits, one character, ads, and limited access.

Pro

Pro is a paid subscription with higher generation limits, up to three characters, saved history, PDF/export tools, and reduced or no ads.

Ultra Premium

Ultra Premium is a paid subscription with access to all available characters, higher limits, beta access, no ads, and limited video generation.

Enterprise

Enterprise is a custom plan for organizations, teams, schools, agencies, and businesses. Enterprise terms, pricing, seats, support, and usage limits may vary by agreement.

Music Production

Music Production is a coming soon paid add-on. It is not included automatically in any plan unless purchased separately or included under an Enterprise agreement.

Billing

Subscriptions may renew automatically unless canceled through the applicable app store or billing provider.

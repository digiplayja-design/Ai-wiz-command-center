Receipt Wiz Free and Full

Receipt Wiz Free includes 20 AI receipt scans per calendar month and up to 100 stored receipts within 250 MB. Manual entry, review, search and CSV export remain available. Receipt Wiz Full is an optional Google Play subscription with a US base price of $8.99 per month, including 1,000 AI receipt scans per calendar month and up to 5,000 stored receipts within 2 GB. Google Play shows the available local price, currency, taxes and renewal details before purchase. Availability depends on the store and country.

Full is included while your KORLIX Pro, Ultra Premium or Enterprise entitlement is active; no additional Receipt Wiz subscription is required. A separate Receipt Wiz Full purchase does not upgrade your main KORLIX plan or unlock other paid tools. If you later obtain an included KORLIX plan, manage and cancel any separate Receipt Wiz subscription in Google Play to avoid further renewals.

Scan allowances are shared across your KORLIX receipt tools and reset on the first day of each calendar month at 00:00 UTC, without rollover. Failed scans return their reserved allowance. Deleting receipts, reinstalling the app or changing plans does not reset completed usage. Receipt scanning does not spend AI GAS or generation credits. Additional uploads stop when either the receipt-count or storage limit is reached; you can export and remove unneeded receipts to make room.

Receipt Wiz Full renews monthly unless canceled in Google Play. Cancellation normally leaves access until the paid period ends; refund, revocation, billing-hold and store rules can affect entitlement. When Full ends, Free limits apply to new uploads and scans. Saved receipts remain accessible for viewing, editing, export and deletion even when above the Free storage allowance. Use Restore purchases with the original Google Play account and the KORLIX account associated with the purchase.

Cancel, manage or restore

For a web subscription, sign in to KORLIX and open Settings → Plans & Billing. Choose Manage Billing to view invoices, update your payment method or change your plan. Choose Cancel renewal to stop future renewals; access remains through the paid period. Stripe displays any prorated charge or credit before you confirm a plan change. Failed or incomplete payments do not extend paid access. Refunded or disputed payments may require a support review before paid access is restored.

If you already subscribe through an app store, manage that subscription with the store. Use the original purchase channel to avoid duplicate billing. On iPhone or iPad, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → KORLIX AI to manage an Apple-billed subscription. See Apple's cancellation instructions. The Apple purchase screen and subscription settings show the applicable renewal and expiry details.

For a Google Play subscription, open Play Store → profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions, select KORLIX AI and follow the cancellation instructions. You can also open Google Play subscriptions. Use the same Google account that purchased access. If another provider billed you, manage the subscription with that provider.

Cancellation normally stops the next renewal; your checkout/provider terms determine when access ends. Uninstalling KORLIX, signing out or requesting account deletion does not automatically cancel billing. If purchased access is missing, confirm the purchasing account and contact support with a transaction reference, never full card details.

For an eligible Apple subscription or non-consumable purchase, sign in with the Apple Account used to purchase and the correct KORLIX account, then use Restore Purchases in the app's Apple billing screen. See Apple's restore guidance. Restore is not a new purchase, does not cancel a subscription and does not replenish spent consumable credits. Do not buy again merely to resolve a missing entitlement; contact support with the receipt reference if restoration fails. Google Play and web purchases use their original billing account and the KORLIX account associated with the purchase.

Allowances, credits and media

Generation allowances may have daily or monthly limits and reset rules shown in the app. Different features can consume different amounts. There is no general promise that unused allowance rolls over or that a deleted result refunds completed usage.

AI GAS is a separately purchased allowance for eligible voice use, not a bank balance, investment or cryptocurrency. Review the supported uses, amount, price and any applicable limits shown before buying. Purchasing AI GAS does not by itself unlock a paid feature or replace its plan requirement. Feature-specific generation allowances, LIVE CONVO allowances and Music Production creation allowances are distinct; do not assume one can be used in place of another. Your account's usage screen and the accepted offer govern the allowance supplied.

Music requests reserve allowance while submitting and consume it when the provider accepts the request. An uncertain submission can remain reserved until its status is resolved; use the recovery/retry control rather than start duplicate requests. See Music Production terms.

For web billing or refund requests, contact support@korlixdeveloper.com with your account email and transaction reference. Cancellation does not automatically refund a completed billing period. Requests are reviewed against the circumstances and applicable consumer rights. Do not send full card numbers, security codes or passwords.

Refund eligibility depends on applicable law and the billing provider's rules. For Google Play purchases use Google Play's refund process; contact support if you need help identifying a purchase or a missing entitlement. No statement here removes statutory refund or consumer rights.

For Apple-billed purchases, use Apple's Report a Problem service and refund instructions. Apple determines eligibility under its rules and applicable law; KORLIX cannot promise an Apple or Google refund. An approved refund, charge reversal or revoked purchase may remove the associated entitlement or unused allowance as applicable.

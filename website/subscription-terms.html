Billing and renewal

For purchases billed by Google Play, Google Play's checkout, account and billing terms apply. Other eligible purchase channels have their own terms. Availability of a plan on the web does not establish that the same purchase route is available inside the Android app.

Cancel, manage or restore

For a Google Play subscription, open Play Store → profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions, select KORLIX AI and follow the cancellation instructions. You can also open Google Play subscriptions. Use the same Google account that purchased access. If another provider billed you, manage the subscription with that provider.

Cancellation normally stops the next renewal; your checkout/provider terms determine when access ends. Uninstalling KORLIX, signing out or requesting account deletion does not automatically cancel billing. If purchased access is missing, confirm the purchasing account and contact support with a transaction reference, never full card details.

Allowances, credits and media

Music or video access can require a separate entitlement and is subject to provider availability, duration and content restrictions. There is no general promise that unused allowance rolls over or that a deleted result refunds completed usage.

Music requests reserve allowance while submitting and consume it when the provider accepts the request. An uncertain submission can remain reserved until its status is resolved; use the recovery/retry control rather than start duplicate requests. See Music Production terms.

Refund eligibility depends on applicable law and the billing provider's rules. For Google Play purchases use Google Play's refund process; contact support if you need help identifying a purchase or a missing entitlement. No statement here removes statutory refund or consumer rights.

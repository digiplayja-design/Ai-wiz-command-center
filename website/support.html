Contact Korlix Developer for help with KORLIX AI at support@korlixdeveloper.com.

Choose a subject

  • Account or technical support: include your app version, platform, feature and a description of the problem.
  • Billing: include the billing provider and transaction reference, never full card details.
  • Delete Korlix AI Account: follow the account and data deletion instructions.
  • Privacy request: identify the information or choice you need help with.
  • AI Safety Report: use in-app Report Output or identify the relevant result.
  • Community Report: use Social report/block controls or identify the relevant member/content.
  • Child Safety — Korlix AI: follow the Child Safety Standards reporting instructions.
  • Moderation review: include the decision/account reference and why you request review.
  • Enterprise: describe the organization feature or agreement concerned.

Protect sensitive information

Do not send passwords, login codes, recovery phrases, full payment-card information or suspected illegal images/videos. Redact sensitive content from screenshots. We may ask you to verify account ownership before discussing or changing private records.

Urgent safety concerns

Contact local emergency services for immediate danger. KORLIX support is not an emergency channel and does not promise continuous real-time monitoring.
